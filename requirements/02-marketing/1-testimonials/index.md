# Epic: Testimonials

**Code:** EPC-02.1
**Domain:** Marketing (DOM-02)
**Description:** Show curated client testimonials as social proof on marketing
surfaces.

**Created:** 2026-07-28 | **Last Updated:** 2026-07-28

---

## Overview

Prospective clients trust peers more than they trust marketing copy.
Testimonials put real client voices in front of visitors at the moments they are
evaluating the company. The **content type** for capturing testimonials as
structured, curated content is built and live; surfacing a testimonial on a
marketing surface is tracked separately as ticket work.

## Epic Scope

**In Scope:**
- Testimonials as structured, reusable content
- Presenting a curated testimonial on a marketing surface

**Out of Scope:**
- Collecting testimonials from clients via a form
- Star ratings or review aggregation
- Automatic rotation/carousel behavior
